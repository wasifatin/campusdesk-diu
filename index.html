About CampusDesk & Technical Disclosures

CampusDesk is an interactive university help desk designed with automated priority escalation, grounded AI assistance, and transparent lifecycle tracking.

System Architecture & Features:

  • Local Storage Scope: Reports persist securely on your machine in campusdesk_records_live. No external data telemetry.
  • Automated Keyword Escalation: Words indicating danger (e.g. fire, sparks, exposed wire, threat) immediately trigger Critical emergency dispatch.
  • Offline Capabilities: Built with local standalone assets, enabling inspection, chat, and triage even when offline.
